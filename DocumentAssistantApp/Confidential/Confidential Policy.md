# Confidential Document Handling Policy

> Placeholder sample bundled with the app. Replace this file with the official

## 1. Purpose

This policy describes how confidential documents are stored, accessed, and
reviewed within the organization.

## 2. Scope

Applies to every employee, contractor, and system that handles material marked
Confidential.

## 3. Handling Rules

- Confidential material must never leave the approved on-device storage.
- Access is granted on a strict need-to-know basis.
- Copies must not be exported, emailed, or uploaded to third-party services.
- Any suspected exposure must be reported to the security team immediately.

## 4. Review

This policy is reviewed annually, or whenever a material change to handling
occurs.
