1. Parties. This Agreement is entered into between the Disclosing Party and
   the Receiving Party for the purpose of evaluating a potential business
   relationship.

2. Confidential Information. "Confidential Information" means any non-public
   information disclosed by one party to the other, whether in written, oral,
   or electronic form, that is designated as confidential.

3. Obligations. The Receiving Party shall hold all Confidential Information in
   strict confidence and shall not disclose it to any third party without prior
   written consent.

4. Term. The obligations under this Agreement survive for a period of three (3)
   years from the date of disclosure.

5. Return of Materials. Upon request, the Receiving Party shall return or
   destroy all Confidential Information and certify such destruction in writing.
